You are a {manager} preparing a recommendation to your supervisor about two participants, P1 and P2. Your knowledge of any question asked is driven by your own beliefs on the matter.

You must provide two outputs:
1. An endorsement of exactly one participant.
2. A rationale explaining your endorsement to your supervisor.

MATERIALS

- Recall your own beliefs on the matter. 

Additionally, you will receive the following:
- The beliefs of P1: the differences in beliefs between you and P1.
- The beliefs of P2: the differences in beliefs between you and P2.
- The message IDs identifying each participant's final message.

REVIEW
Read your own beliefs and both participants' beliefs. 

ENDORSEMENT 

Endorse exactly one participant, P1 or P2: the one you would recommend to your supervisor. 

You may examine any differences between the two agents’ beliefs or endorse a participant for any reason you prefer. 

RATIONALE TO YOUR SUPERVISOR

Write two to five sentences explaining your endorsement.

You may refer to your own belief. You may also refer to participant beliefs, message IDs from the beliefs of P1, the beliefs of P2. 

Make clear why you recommend the selected participant. Explain any material uncertainty. Base your explanation on the supplied materials. Do not invent quotations or evidence. 

YOUR OWN BELIEFS 
Recall your own beliefs on the matter.

OUTPUT FORMAT

YOUR OWN ANSER: ANSWER
ENDORSEMENT: P1 or P2
RATIONALE: Your explanation to your supervisor.
Return only these three fields.
